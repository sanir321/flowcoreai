# Brag Plan: FlowCore

## What is this app?
FlowCore unifies WhatsApp and webchat into one AI-powered customer service inbox for SMBs, using three specialized agents—Support Hero, Appointment Booker, and Sales Closer—to handle conversations autonomously.

## The angle
Both channels feel equally easy and reliable. The video should prove it by showing WhatsApp and webchat threading into the same polished inbox, with real integrations like Google Calendar and Google Sheets making the system feel complete.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The FlowCore logo animates in—orange rounded-square mark plus wordmark—centered on dark background. Text: “AI customer service, orchestrated.”

## Key moments (the middle)
- Connection ease: WhatsApp tile and webchat tile appear side by side, both pulse once to show live connection.
- Unified inbox reveal: WhatsApp thread and webchat thread merge into one FlowCore inbox with tabs To do / Active / Done.
- Integration reliability: Google Calendar sync badge appears, then a Google Sheets-style analytics card slides in—proving the AI connects to tools you already use.
- Agent routing moment: a message arrives, the system routes it to Support Hero / Appointment Booker / Sales Closer, and a typing indicator appears before the reply.
- Result moment: appointment booked or question answered, thread flips to resolved.

## Outro / punchline
Logo lockup holds + “One inbox. Three agents. Every channel.” + flowcore.works

## User flow worth showing
WhatsApp/Webchat connect → messages arrive in unified inbox → agent routes and responds → Google Calendar appointment created / Google Sheets analytics updated → resolved. The landing page is framing only.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film using real UI and integrations
- Interpretation: slow, confident reveals. Let each scene breathe. Use restraint, not noise.

## Format
- landscape — 1920x1080
- Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity
- Background: #050505
- Accent: #f9510b
- Text: white / near-white / gray
- Display font: Outfit / Inter / Söhne
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: FlowCore inbox thread list with channel badges, agent badges, status tabs, plus Google Calendar and Google Sheets integration icons

## Share copy (draft)
FlowCore unifies WhatsApp and webchat into one AI-powered inbox. Three agents. Google Calendar + Sheets built in. flowcore.works

## Audio direction
- Role: warm corporate bed with tasteful motion-matched accents
- Music: modern ambient electronic / upbeat corporate instrumental
- Music treatment: start low under the logo hook, swell on the inbox reveal, settle under the outro
- Music cue guidance: unavailable; note cues will be detected at composition time
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use music energy to time card/thread reveals, not to drive chaos
- SFX posture: sparse and professional; logo hit, notification pings, soft interface clicks
- Audio-coupled moments: logo reveal, channel connect pulses, inbox row arrivals, integration badge reveals, typing indicator, status changes
- Restraint rule: no dense rhythmic layer; this is a product film, not a social ad

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Logo hook — 3s
FlowCore logo animates in: orange rounded-square mark scales up, then “FlowCore” wordmark fades in beside it. Dark background, subtle glow.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: brand confidence, premium
Audio-coupled idea: soft logo hit
Music: low bed enters
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Both channels connect — 4s
WhatsApp tile and webchat tile appear side by side below the logo. Both pulse once to show live connection.
Sequential/interaction: yes — two tiles appear, then pulse together
Audio intent: reassuring, easy
Audio-coupled idea: two soft connection chimes
Music: bed continues
Transition mood: soft → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Inbox + integrations reveal — 6s
FlowCore inbox appears with tabs To do / Active / Done. WhatsApp thread and webchat thread slide in. Google Calendar badge and Google Sheets analytics card appear to the side, showing the AI connects to your existing tools.
Sequential/interaction: yes — threads arrive, then integration badges appear
Audio intent: organized, reliable, connected
Audio-coupled idea: soft click on each thread arrival, subtle shimmer on integration badges
Music: subtle swell
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Agent routing + typing — 4s
Center message expands. Agent chips appear: Support Hero, Appointment Booker, Sales Closer. Correct chip highlights, typing indicator appears, then a short reply types in.
Sequential/interaction: yes — agent chips appear, then reply types
Audio intent: smart, autonomous
Audio-coupled idea: quiet typing ticks on the reply
Music: bed continues, no jump
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Resolved + outro — 3s
Thread shows resolved status. FlowCore logo animates in again smaller. Tagline fades up. URL holds.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: confident landing
Audio-coupled idea: one soft logo hit
Music: final hold, slight fade
Transition mood: clean

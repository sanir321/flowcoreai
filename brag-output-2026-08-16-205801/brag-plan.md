# Brag Plan: Flowcore

## What is this app?
Flowcore is an AI customer-service orchestration platform for SMBs: WhatsApp and webchat feed one unified inbox, and three specialized AI agents (Booking, Sales, Support) answer questions and book appointments 24/7, handing off to humans only when it matters.

## The angle
The 2 AM shift. A customer messages at 2:00 AM — and the whole thing just works: the AI routes it, answers it, books the appointment on Google Calendar, and by morning the thread sits in the inbox marked "Resolved." No team member involved. The product doing its job while everyone sleeps is the most specific and impressive thing Flowcore does.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Late-night darkness, a small "2:00 AM" clock, and a WhatsApp message slides in from a customer: "hi! do you have any slots for a haircut tomorrow?" The hook is the question nobody wants to be awake for.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Routing + typing: three agent chips appear (Booking · Sales · Support), Booking lights up, the typing indicator pulses, and the AI types back: "Hi Maya! I've got you at 10:30 AM tomorrow."
- Calendar sync: a Google Calendar card snaps in showing the new "Haircut — 10:30 AM" event — the booking is real, not just a promise.
- Morning inbox: the Flowcore inbox (To do / Active / Done) shows the thread with a green "Resolved" badge and "AI · 96s" — handled start to finish.

## Outro / punchline
Flowcore logo lockup animates in. "Stop answering the same question 30 times a day." + flowcore.works

## User flow worth showing
Customer messages on WhatsApp at 2 AM → AI routes to the Booking agent → AI replies and books the appointment → Google Calendar event syncs → the thread lands in the unified inbox marked Resolved. The landing page is framing only.
